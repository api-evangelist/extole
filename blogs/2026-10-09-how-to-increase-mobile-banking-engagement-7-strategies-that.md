What Is Mobile Banking Engagement (and Why Does It Matter)? In sort, here is how to increase mobile banking engagement: simplify the app, onboard customers well, personalize guidance, send smart alerts, and add in-app rewards. Together, these steps give customers clear reasons to open the app, engage with more of it, and keep returning.
